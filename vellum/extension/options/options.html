How do I apply a theme?

Click the Vellum icon in your toolbar while on claude.ai or chatgpt.com. Tap any theme to apply it instantly.

My theme looks washed out?

Switch Claude or ChatGPT to dark mode in their appearance settings. Vellum's themes are crafted for dark mode.
